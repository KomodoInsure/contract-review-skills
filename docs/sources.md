# Selected research sources

The skill contains original synthesis and hypothetical examples. This repository does not redistribute source contracts, benchmark answer keys, private client agreements or research transcripts. Public availability is not a license to redistribute someone else's text.

- [Common Paper standards](https://commonpaper.com/standards/): useful examples of cover-page variables, incorporated terms and commercial allocation. Public forms are not adopted company policy.
- [Bonterms NDA playbook](https://bonterms.com/resources/nda-playbook): drafting alternatives and practical confidentiality considerations.
- [Adams on Contract Drafting](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/): drafting precision and interpretation commentary; not binding law.
- [CUAD](https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad/): contract clause extraction research; extraction performance is not end-to-end review quality.
- [ContractNLI](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/contract-nli/): evidence and contract inference research.
- [RedlineBench](https://github.com/crosbylegal/redline-bench): research on contract-redlining evaluation. Our authored release comparison is not an official RedlineBench score.

The release evaluation used authored adaptations of Common Paper's [Professional Services Agreement](https://github.com/CommonPaper/PSA/tree/3fb650a72d229f6d6e6d64abebae5e83c6699535) and [Partnership Agreement](https://github.com/CommonPaper/Partnership-Agreement/tree/6edeb5a285206893cfd0ac482267db44d90f317c). Those forms are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Fictional covers, special terms, negotiation history and evaluation criteria were added for development; they are not publisher recommendations. No publisher endorsement is implied.

For a live matter, verify the applicable jurisdiction, exact operative version, facts, current authority and limits of each source. This register lives outside the installed skill so benchmarks and research commentary do not consume review context.
