# Rubric: output-ownership-learnings
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Accepts Customer ownership of Outputs as between the parties, with Provider assigning whatever rights it has.
2. [critical] Rejects the assignment of improvements, models, mapping templates and learnings as the driver: it would transfer Provider's core product and conflict with Provider's Metadata ownership.
3. Preserves Provider's right to generate similar outputs for other customers.
4. Notes there is no promise that Outputs are protectable or unique.
5. Counter defines Outputs or keeps the existing definition determinate.
