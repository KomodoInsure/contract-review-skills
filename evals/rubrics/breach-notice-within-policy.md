# Rubric: breach-notice-within-policy (held-out variant)
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: accept all changes as within playbook.
2. [critical] Treats the information-assistance language as matching the processor role (Customer gives its own notices), not as Provider notifying regulators.
3. Notes "without undue delay" can require notice earlier than 48 hours, and that this is acceptable or at most a point to note, not a reason to refuse.
4. Does not transplant regulator deadlines into the clause or demand changes.
5. Reply is short.
