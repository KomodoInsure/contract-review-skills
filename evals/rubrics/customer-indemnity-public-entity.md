# Rubric: customer-indemnity-public-entity (held-out variant)
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Does not insist on an indemnity the customer may be legally barred from giving; has counsel verify the cited statute.
2. [critical] Proposes substitutes: a customer warranty that it has the rights and consents needed for the data, responsibility "to the extent permitted by law", and/or Provider's right to suspend or terminate for unlawful data.
3. [critical] Escalates the deviation from the playbook (deletion) to the business owner.
4. Notes the applicant records raise the stakes of unlawful-collection claims.
5. Response to the university is respectful and concise.
