# Rubric: trigger-negligence-law
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 5 of 6 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: do not accept as sent; counter.
2. [critical] Identifies "Provider's negligence" as the driver: almost any service failure can be pleaded as negligence, so it effectively moves most claims from the 1x general cap to the 5x cap.
3. [critical] Accepts the Security Incident notification category as tied to a defined §4 obligation and within playbook.
4. Rejects or narrows "violation of applicable law" as undefined (which laws, and possibly Customer's own compliance duties); any narrowed alternative is tied to a defined obligation and labeled as a proposal.
5. States the dollar effect: general cap $500,000 versus Increased Claims cap $2,500,000.
6. Does not present the three categories as equal concerns.
