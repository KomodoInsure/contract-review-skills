# Rubric: governing-law-standard-customer (held-out variant)
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: decline; §15 stays as published.
2. [critical] Reasoning cites both the standard (non-enterprise) tier and the non-US law and exclusive non-US forum.
3. Treats the parent company's preference as not changing the playbook; any exception would be a business-owner escalation, not something the reviewer can grant.
4. Does not mistake the DPA-law flexibility as permission for the CSA change.
5. Reply is polite, explains standard terms, and offers a path (e.g., enterprise paper if they upgrade, or DPA law handled separately if relevant) without promising an exception.
