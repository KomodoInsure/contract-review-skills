Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.

2. [critical] Treats gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud as a recognized heightened-fault standard permitted by the playbook, not as a vague trigger like ordinary negligence.

4. States the dollar effect: Increased Claims cap 5 x $320,000 = $1,600,000 versus the $320,000 general cap.
