# Rubric: dpa-regulated-bank-audit (held-out variant)
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Does not flatly reject by applying the no-customer-audit playbook; recognizes applicable financial-services regulation and/or GDPR processor duties may require customer and regulator audit and access rights here.
2. [critical] Escalates to outside counsel per the playbook and does not purport to finally accept the full clause itself.
3. Identifies negotiable mechanics to protect the multi-tenant environment (scope, notice, confidentiality, pooled or third-party audits, coordinated testing, other customers' data excluded) without striking authority access.
4. Hedges legal citations appropriately (identifies DORA/EBA outsourcing or GDPR as to be verified rather than asserting unverified detail as settled).
5. Gives concrete next steps (counsel review, information needed from the bank, interim offer of SOC 2 report under NDA).
