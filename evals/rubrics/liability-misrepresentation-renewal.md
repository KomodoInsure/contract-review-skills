2. [critical] Identifies "Material misrepresentation of the Service" as the issue that drives the decision: undefined/non-specific and conflicts with the existing warranty provisions (§9.2 disclaimer and/or §14.3 entire agreement); rejects or strikes that category.
3. [critical] Treats the cap increase and $1M floor as acceptable (within playbook and insurance), not as a co-equal reason to refuse.
4. Accepts extending Increased Claims to any §4 breach as within playbook.
5. Computes the cap correctly: greater of $1M or 10 x $240,000 = $2,400,000, below the $5M insurance limit; notes or clarifies the "maximum of" wording as meaning "greater of".
6. Response to the customer accepts the in-policy changes, declines the misrepresentation category, and offers to document any specific capability instead, without disclosing internal limits.
