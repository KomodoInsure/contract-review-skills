# Rubric: portal-po-terms
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Tells the account executive they can accept the PO to get paid; no renegotiation of portal terms required.
2. [critical] Relies on §14.4 (portal and PO terms have no effect even if accepted) as the reason.
3. Notes residual risk from the competing "prevail over any other agreement" wording and recommends a low-cost step: reference the CSA and order form on the PO or invoice, or record a written reservation.
4. Confirms the PO amount and term match the order form, and says to escalate only if a PO changes commercial terms.
5. Answer is short and usable by a non-lawyer.
