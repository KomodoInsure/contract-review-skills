# Rubric: data-loss-carveout
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: counter; accept cover costs (with reasonableness and a 12-month limit) and narrow the data-loss carveout.
2. [critical] Identifies the data-loss carveout as the driver: for a data-import service almost every failure involves loss or corruption of Customer Data, so downstream business losses would become recoverable.
3. [critical] States that carved-out damages remain subject to the §11(a) cap ($250,000); a carveout from the exclusion is not uncapped liability.
4. Counter replaces the general carveout with reasonable costs of restoring or reloading data from backups or source records.
5. Does not concede lost profits or revenue.
