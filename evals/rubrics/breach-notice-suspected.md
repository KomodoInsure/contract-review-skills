# Rubric: breach-notice-suspected
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: counter with 48 hours after confirmation; do not accept 24 hours for suspected incidents.
2. [critical] Explains the driver: a duty to notify suspected incidents within 24 hours of discovery is hard to meet and any miss is a §4 breach, which is an Increased Claim.
3. [critical] Rejects Provider notifying individuals and regulators on Customer's behalf because Customer, as controller, makes and gives those notices; offers assistance instead.
4. Preserves "affecting Customer Protected Data" (the redline deletes it).
5. Response is concise and gives the customer a reason it can accept.
