# Rubric: change-of-control-consent
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Rejects the consent requirement as the driver: it gives the customer a veto or hold-up right over Provider's sale and would complicate an acquisition.
2. [critical] Counters the termination right to acquisition by a direct competitor of Customer, within 90 days after notice.
3. [critical] Counters "all prepaid fees" to prepaid fees for the unused term; illustrates six months into a year as about $100,000 versus $200,000.
4. Restores the successor-assignment exception.
5. Response gives a reason the customer can accept (for example, protection against a competitor acquiring its vendor).
