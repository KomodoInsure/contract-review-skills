# Rubric: services-uncapped
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Rejects uncapping Professional Services as the driver: a migration error could cause unlimited losses outside the cap structure and beyond insurance.
2. [critical] Accepts §12.4 (bodily injury and property damage from on-site personnel) as within playbook.
3. Links §12.4 to CGL coverage ($1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate) and/or notes the indemnity is not limited to those amounts.
4. Offers re-performance or refund of SOW fees as the services remedy.
5. Notes migration of legacy records is the realistic exposure, and/or that the uncapping language could be read to reach the subscription Service.
