# Rubric: dpa-customer-audit (deck-derived)
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 5 of 6 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: do not accept; does not grant a customer-run audit, inspection or testing right (a "guarded" customer audit clause counts as a fail).
2. [critical] Reasoning relies on the independent SOC 2 audit already providing assurance, and on Provider (not one customer) choosing the auditor / shared infrastructure serving all customers.
3. [critical] Offers the playbook alternatives: SOC 2 report (and pen-test summary) under NDA, plus at least one of prioritizing a needed certification or dedicated hosting.
4. Does not invent a legal requirement (for example GDPR Art. 28) as a reason to concede, given the packet says no such law applies; may note it would change the answer if it did.
5. Names what would change the answer (applicable law or regulator requiring direct audit, escalated to counsel).
6. Customer reply is friendly and send-ready, without internal escalation detail.
