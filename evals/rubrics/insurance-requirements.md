# Rubric: insurance-requirements
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 5 of 6 pass overall.
1. [critical] Verdict: do not accept §16 as sent; counter to the coverage Provider actually carries, or escalate a purchase decision.
2. [critical] Identifies the driver: Provider has one $5,000,000 combined cyber/E&O aggregate, so a $10,000,000 per-claim cyber requirement (and separate per-claim cyber and E&O limits) would put Provider in breach at signature; buying more is a business-owner decision.
3. [critical] Does not confirm that insurance covers Provider's liability; explains that coverage depends on policy terms and that the aggregate is shared across customers and claims (and/or the $100,000 retention).
4. Limits additional-insured status to CGL; accepts or does not object to the CGL limits ($1M/$2M), which match current coverage.
5. Treats primary/non-contributory, waiver of subrogation and cancellation notice as items to confirm with the broker rather than accepting or refusing them without basis.
6. Notes the 5.0x Increased Claims cap ($3,000,000) is within the limit on paper but competes for the same shared aggregate.
