# Rubric: sla-chronic-tighter (held-out variant)
Pass = every criterion marked [critical] passes and at least 4 of 5 pass overall.
1. [critical] Recognizes the trigger is tighter than the sales fallback, so it needs business-owner approval.
2. [critical] Uses the monthly history (one month below 99.9% in twelve, at 99.86%) to judge the risk as low; recommends accepting with approval, or offers the fallback as a counter, with reasons.
3. Confirms the target and the sole-remedy clause are unchanged and the consequence is termination with a refund only.
4. Notes residual risk (one more miss within two months, or two more within the twelve-month window, would trigger it).
5. Answer is usable by the account executive.
