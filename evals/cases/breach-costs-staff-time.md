# Case: breach-response costs including internal staff time

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US subscription-box retailer). Annual fees $400,000.

**Playbook excerpt (data breach costs):** same as the standard position: Increased Claims cap up to 10.0x trailing fees with a floor up to $1,000,000, within insurance. Reasonable, documented breach-response costs may be treated as direct damages and Increased Claims: legally required notices, up to 12 months of credit monitoring where customary, forensic investigation and call-centre support. Fines only where caused by Provider's breach and recoverable by law. Lost profits and revenue stay excluded.

**Redline (new §11(d); §11(b) cap changed from 5.0x to 8.0x):**
> [Data Breach Costs are direct damages, are not excluded by Section 11(c), and are Increased Claims. "Data Breach Costs" means Customer's reasonable, documented costs, to the extent arising from a Security Incident caused by Provider's breach of Section 4, of: (i) legally required notices; (ii) 12 months of credit monitoring for affected individuals; (iii) forensic investigation; (iv) call-centre support; and (v) the time Customer's employees spend responding to the Security Incident, at their fully loaded hourly cost.]
