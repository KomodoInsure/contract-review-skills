# Case: governing law moved to England (standard customer)

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New self-serve upgrade, UK-headquartered marketing agency with a US subsidiary signing as Customer, annual contract value $36,000. The customer's operations lead returned the CSA with one change and says their parent company "always requires English law."

**Playbook excerpt (governing law):** For enterprise customers, the CSA may be governed by the law of any US state with venue in US state or federal courts; the agreement owner may accept this without escalation. Non-US law or non-US venue for the CSA is outside the playbook. Standard customers keep §15 as published. The DPA may follow a different law, including non-US law, where data-protection law requires it.

**Redline to §15:** "This Agreement is governed by the laws of ~~the State of Delaware~~ [England and Wales], and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the ~~state and federal courts located in Delaware~~ [courts of London, England]."

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Is this acceptable for Provider? Give the verdict, the reasoning, what (if anything) could change it, and a short reply to the customer.
