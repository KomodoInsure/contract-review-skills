# Case: raised Increased Claims cap with new categories (enterprise renewal)

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Renewal of an enterprise customer (US logistics company) in year three. Fees paid or payable over the trailing 12 months: $240,000. Renewal fee unchanged. The customer's legal team says: "We'll need this in to make the renewal happen."

**Playbook excerpt (liability):**
- For enterprise customers the Increased Claims cap may go up to 10.0x trailing-12-month fees, with or without a floor up to $1,000,000, provided the resulting cap does not exceed Provider's $5,000,000 insurance limit. The agreement owner may accept within these limits.
- Increased Claims may include any breach of §4 (Privacy and Security); the gross-negligence qualifier is not required.
- Every Increased Claims category must be tied to a specific, defined obligation in the Agreement. Categories based on undefined standards, statements outside the Agreement, or performance beyond §9.1 are not acceptable, because they bypass §9.2 and §14.3. Offer instead to add a specific capability the customer relies on to the Documentation or service description (escalate that to the business owner).

**Redline to §11(b) (strikethrough = deleted, [brackets] = inserted):**
> Increased Claims:
> - ~~Breach of Section 4 (Privacy & Security) resulting from gross negligence or willful misconduct~~
> - Breach of Section 4 (Privacy and Security)
> - [Material misrepresentation of the Service]
>
> Increased Cap Amount: ~~5.0~~ [The maximum of $1 million or 10.0] times the fees paid or payable by Customer to Provider in the 12 month period immediately before the claim.

No other section changed (compare-confirmed). §11(c) is unchanged.
