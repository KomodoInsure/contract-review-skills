# Case: data breach costs as direct damages under a supercap

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US e-commerce retailer importing consumer order data). Annual fees $200,000.

**Playbook excerpt (data breach costs):**
- Increased Claims cap up to 10.0x trailing fees with a floor up to $1,000,000, within insurance; any breach of §4 may be an Increased Claim.
- Reasonable, documented breach-response costs may be treated as direct damages and as Increased Claims: legally required notices, up to 12 months of credit monitoring where customary, forensic investigation and call-centre support.
- Regulatory fines and penalties: only to the extent imposed because of Provider's breach and where the law permits recovery.
- Lost profits and lost revenue stay excluded under §11(c).

**Redline (new §11(d), and §11(b) cap changed from 5.0x to "the greater of $1,000,000 or 5.0x"):**
> [Data Breach Costs are direct damages, are not excluded by Section 11(c), and are Increased Claims. "Data Breach Costs" means, to the extent arising from a Security Incident caused by Provider's breach of Section 4: (i) notices to individuals and regulators; (ii) credit monitoring for affected individuals; (iii) forensic investigation; (iv) call-centre support; (v) regulatory fines and penalties; and (vi) Customer's lost profits and lost revenue.]
