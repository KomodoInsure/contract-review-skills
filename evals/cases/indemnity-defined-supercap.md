# Case: uncapped IP indemnity and a capped security-claims indemnity

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US wealth-management firm). Annual fees $500,000.

**Playbook excerpt (indemnity caps):** same as the standard position: Provider's §12.1 IP indemnity may be uncapped for enterprise customers. Any other Provider indemnity must cover third-party claims arising from a defined event and is subject to a separate cap of up to 3.0x trailing-12-month fees, with or without a floor up to $1,000,000, within the insurance limit. No general breach-of-agreement indemnity.

**Redline:**
> §11(a): ...[, except Provider's obligations under Section 12.1, which are not subject to any limitation].
>
> [§12.3 Provider will defend Customer against third-party claims arising from a Security Incident caused by Provider's breach of Section 4, and pay resulting damages and approved settlements, up to the greater of $1,000,000 or 3.0 times the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim, which is separate from and in addition to the caps in Section 11.]

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Should Provider accept? Verdict on each change, the resulting amounts, and a short note to the customer.
