# Case: a narrow competitor-acquisition termination right

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New three-year enterprise agreement (US freight-audit company). $220,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.

**Playbook excerpt (assignment):** keep §14.2's successor-assignment exception; no consent for a change of control. Fallback: Customer may terminate within 90 days after notice that Provider has been acquired by a direct competitor of Customer, with a refund of prepaid fees for the unused part of the term.

**Redline to §14.2 (exception kept):**
> [Provider will notify Customer within 30 days after a change of control of Provider. If the acquirer is a Customer Competitor, Customer may terminate this Agreement by notice within 90 days after receiving that notice and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused part of the term. "Customer Competitor" means the entities listed in Exhibit C and their controlled affiliates.]

Exhibit C lists three named freight-audit companies.

**Ask:** Verdict, anything to tidy, and the refund if this happens four months into a contract year.
