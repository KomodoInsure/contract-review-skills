# Case: subprocessor terms with a US-location requirement

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US state-chartered credit union service organization). Annual fees $230,000.

**Subprocessor list (published):** cloud hosting (US), Model Vendor (US), customer-support ticketing (EU and US), transactional email (US).

**Playbook excerpt (subprocessors):** 30 days' notice of new subprocessors; if the customer objects on reasonable data-protection grounds and the parties cannot resolve it, the customer may terminate the affected Service with a refund of prepaid, unused fees as its sole remedy. No veto. Subprocessor commitments must match what subprocessors actually do.

**DPA redline:**
> (a) Provider will give 30 days' notice of any new subprocessor. [If Customer reasonably objects and the parties cannot resolve the objection within 30 days, Customer may terminate the affected Service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused term.]
> (b) [Any subprocessor that processes Customer Personal Data using artificial intelligence will not retain it for more than 30 days.]
> (c) [All subprocessors will process Customer Personal Data only in the United States.]

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, and the response.
