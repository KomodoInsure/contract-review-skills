# Case: Increased Claims cap above the approved multiple (enterprise renewal)

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Renewal of an enterprise customer (US payroll software company) in year two. Fees paid or payable over the trailing 12 months: $400,000; renewal fee rises to $440,000.

**Playbook excerpt (liability):**
- For enterprise customers the Increased Claims cap may go up to 10.0x trailing-12-month fees, with or without a floor up to $1,000,000, provided the resulting cap does not exceed Provider's $5,000,000 insurance limit. The agreement owner may accept within these limits; anything above escalates to the business owner.
- Increased Claims may include any breach of §4 (Privacy and Security); the gross-negligence qualifier is not required.
- Every Increased Claims category must be tied to a specific, defined obligation in the Agreement.

**Redline to §11(b):**
> Increased Claims:
> - ~~Breach of Section 4 (Privacy & Security) resulting from gross negligence or willful misconduct~~
> - Breach of Section 4 (Privacy and Security)[, including Provider's Security Incident notification obligations in Section 4]
>
> Increased Cap Amount: [The greater of $1,000,000 or] ~~5.0~~ [12.0] times the fees paid or payable by Customer to Provider in the 12 month period immediately before the claim.

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Should Provider accept? Give the verdict on each change, identify what drives the decision, compute the resulting cap, and draft a short response.
