# Case: documenting a relied-on AI capability instead of a warranty

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US distributor). Annual fees $190,000. After Provider declined to incorporate its RFP response, the customer proposes this instead.

**Playbook excerpt (warranties):** §9 is non-negotiable. The agreement owner may add a warranty that Provider will comply with laws applicable to Provider in providing the Service. A specific capability the customer relies on may be added to the Documentation or service description with business-owner approval.

**Redline:**
> §9.1 ...[Provider will comply with laws applicable to Provider in providing the Service.]
>
> [Order form, Service Description: "Smart Mapping suggests column mappings that a user reviews and accepts before they apply. Provider measures suggestion acceptance rates and will share its most recent aggregate acceptance rate on request."]

§9.2 is unchanged.

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, who must approve what, and the reply.
