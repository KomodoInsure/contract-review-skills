# Case: new Increased Claims categories for negligence and breach of law

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise renewal (US retail chain), year two. Trailing-12-month fees $500,000; renewal fee unchanged.

**Playbook excerpt (liability):** same as the standard liability position: Increased Claims cap up to 10.0x trailing fees (floor up to $1,000,000) within the $5,000,000 insurance limit; any breach of §4 may be an Increased Claim; every Increased Claims category must be tied to a specific, defined obligation in the Agreement. Categories based on undefined standards are not acceptable.

**Redline to §11(b):**
> Increased Claims:
> - Breach of Section 4 (Privacy & Security) resulting from gross negligence or willful misconduct
> - [Provider's breach of its Security Incident notification obligations in Section 4]
> - [Provider's negligence]
> - [Provider's violation of applicable law]
>
> Increased Cap Amount: 5.0 times the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim (unchanged).

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, the dollar effect, and a counter.
