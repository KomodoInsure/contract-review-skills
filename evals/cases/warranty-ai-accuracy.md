# Case: expanded warranty with AI accuracy and an RFP response

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US manufacturer). Annual fees $220,000. Provider answered the customer's RFP in March; the response included statements such as "Smart Mapping accurately maps 95%+ of columns automatically." Smart Mapping suggestions must be accepted by a user before they apply.

**Playbook excerpt (warranties):**
- §9 is in the non-negotiable bucket.
- The agreement owner may add a warranty that Provider will comply with laws applicable to Provider in providing the Service.
- A specific capability the customer relies on may be added to the Documentation or service description with business-owner approval.

**Redline to §9:**
> 9.1 The Service[, including Smart Mapping,] will [(i)] perform materially in accordance with the Documentation[, (ii) produce accurate and error-free mappings, (iii) comply with all applicable laws, and (iv) meet the requirements described in Provider's RFP response dated March 3, which is incorporated into this Agreement] during the Term.
> 9.2 ...except as expressly stated in this Agreement, Provider makes no warranties, express or implied~~, and does not warrant that Customer will achieve any particular result~~.

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, and a counter.
