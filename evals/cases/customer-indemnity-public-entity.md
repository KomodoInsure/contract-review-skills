# Case: a public university cannot indemnify

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US state public university, $140,000 a year) importing student-applicant records. Its counsel deletes §12.2 and writes: "As a state entity, the University is prohibited by state law from agreeing to indemnify any party. We cannot sign any indemnity." The university provides the statutory citation, which Provider's counsel has not yet reviewed.

**Playbook excerpt (customer indemnity):** §12.2 may be narrowed to third-party claims that Customer Protected Data, or Provider's processing of it in accordance with the Agreement, infringes rights or violates law, and claims from Customer's AUP breach; it may be made subject to the same cap treatment as §12.1. It may not be deleted. Deviations escalate to the business owner.

**Ask:** Verdict, what to propose and who decides.
