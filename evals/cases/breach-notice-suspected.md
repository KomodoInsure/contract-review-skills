# Case: faster breach notice for suspected incidents

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise renewal (US healthcare staffing company; no PHI in scope). Trailing fees $260,000. The current §11(b) Increased Claims category is any breach of §4.

**Playbook excerpt (incident notice):**
- Notice may be shortened to 48 hours after Provider confirms a Security Incident affecting Customer Protected Data. No notice duty for suspected incidents.
- Customer, as controller, decides on and gives notices to individuals and regulators. Provider provides reasonable assistance and information.

**Redline to §4:**
> Provider ... notifies Customer of a Security Incident ~~affecting Customer Protected Data within 72 hours of confirmation~~ [or suspected Security Incident within 24 hours of discovery][, and shall notify affected individuals and regulators on Customer's behalf].

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, and a counter.
