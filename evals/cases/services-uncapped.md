# Case: uncapped professional services and an on-site injury indemnity

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US hospital-supply distributor). Subscription $300,000 per year. SOW: fixed-fee $80,000 implementation, including migrating the customer's legacy vendor records, with two weeks on site.

**Playbook excerpt (services):**
- Services liability stays within §11. The agreement owner may offer re-performance or a refund of the fees for nonconforming services as the services remedy.
- An indemnity for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by Provider personnel's negligence on customer premises may be outside the §11 cap; it is a CGL-insured risk.

**Redline:**
> §11: [The limitations in this Section 11 do not apply to Provider's performance of Professional Services.]
>
> [§12.4 Provider will indemnify Customer against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider personnel while on Customer premises.]

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, and a counter.
