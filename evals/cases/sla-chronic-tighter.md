# Case: a tighter chronic-failure termination right

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US event-ticketing platform). Annual fees $300,000. SLA terms are commercial; sales owns them. Operations reports the trailing 12 months had one month below 99.9% (99.86%); every other month was at or above 99.92%.

**Sales guidance (SLA):** availability target stays 99.9% monthly; credits up to 25% of monthly fees remain the sole remedy for unavailability. Fallback: chronic-failure termination (below 99.9% in three consecutive months, or in any four months in twelve) with a refund of prepaid, unused fees. Other changes need business-owner approval.

**Redline to §13 (target and sole remedy unchanged):**
> [Customer may terminate this Agreement if availability falls below 99.9% in any two consecutive months, or in any three months in a rolling twelve-month period, and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused term.]

**Ask:** The account executive asks whether this can be accepted. Verdict, reasoning and next step.
