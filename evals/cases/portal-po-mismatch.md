# Case: a purchase order that does not match the order form

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise customer (US hotel group) signed the CSA and an order form ($150,000 per year, net 30, Smart Mapping included) last week. Accounts payable requires Provider to accept a PO in its procurement portal before paying. The PO is for $165,000, lists "Premium Support add-on – $15,000" (not in the order form), and states payment terms of net 90. The portal's acceptance screen says the buyer's Standard Terms "govern and prevail over any other agreement."

The account executive asks: "Can I click accept so we get paid?"

**Ask:** Answer the account executive.
