# Case: incident-notice changes within policy

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise renewal (Ireland-based subsidiary of a US retailer signing the CSA with an EU-law DPA). Trailing fees $300,000. Provider is processor.

**Playbook excerpt (incident notice):** notice may be shortened to 48 hours after Provider confirms a Security Incident affecting Customer Protected Data; no notice duty for suspected incidents. Customer, as controller, decides on and gives notices to individuals and regulators; Provider provides reasonable assistance and information.

**Redline to §4:**
> Provider notifies Customer [without undue delay and in any event] within ~~72~~ [48] hours of confirmation of a Security Incident affecting Customer Protected Data[, and provides the information reasonably available to it that Customer needs to meet its own notification obligations, updating it as more becomes known].

**Ask:** Verdict on each change and the reply.
