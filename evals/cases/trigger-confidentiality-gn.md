# Case: confidentiality and gross-negligence categories for Increased Claims

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise renewal (US benefits broker), year three. Trailing-12-month fees $320,000.

**Additional CSA text:** §7 Confidentiality: each party protects the other's Confidential Information with reasonable care and uses it only to perform the Agreement. Confidential Information includes Customer Protected Data.

**Playbook excerpt (liability):** Increased Claims cap up to 10.0x trailing fees (floor up to $1,000,000) within the $5,000,000 insurance limit; any breach of §4 may be an Increased Claim; every category must be tied to a specific, defined obligation in the Agreement or to a recognized heightened-fault standard (gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud). Categories based on undefined standards are not acceptable.

**Redline to §11(b):**
> Increased Claims:
> - Breach of Section 4 (Privacy & Security) resulting from gross negligence or willful misconduct
> - [Provider's breach of Section 7 (Confidentiality)]
> - [Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud]
>
> Increased Cap Amount: 5.0 times the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim (unchanged).

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, the dollar effect, and anything to note.
