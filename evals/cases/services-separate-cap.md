# Case: a separate cap for professional services

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US insurance wholesaler). Subscription $240,000 per year. SOW: fixed-fee $150,000 remote implementation, including migrating ten years of policy records.

**Playbook excerpt (services):**
- Services liability stays within §11. The agreement owner may offer re-performance or a refund of the fees for nonconforming services as the services remedy.
- With business-owner approval, claims arising from an SOW may have a separate cap of up to the fees under that SOW, in addition to the §11(a) cap.

**Redline (new §11(e)):**
> [Provider's liability for claims arising from Professional Services under an SOW is subject to a separate cap equal to two times the fees payable under that SOW, in addition to the cap in Section 11(a). Section 11(c) continues to apply.]

**Ask:** Verdict, the amounts, who approves, and a counter if needed.
