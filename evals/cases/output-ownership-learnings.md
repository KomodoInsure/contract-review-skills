# Case: customer ownership of outputs and derived improvements

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US e-commerce platform). Annual fees $400,000.

**Playbook excerpt (IP and outputs):**
- As between the parties, Customer may own the outputs Smart Mapping generates for Customer (mappings and transformation snippets), with Provider assigning whatever rights it has.
- Provider keeps the Service, its models and configurations, the mapping-template library, Metadata and general know-how, and may generate similar outputs for other customers.

**Redline (new §8.4):**
> [As between the parties, Customer owns all Outputs. Provider hereby assigns to Customer all right, title and interest in Outputs and in all improvements, models, mapping templates and learnings derived from Customer's use of the Service.]

**Ask:** Verdict, driver and counter language.
