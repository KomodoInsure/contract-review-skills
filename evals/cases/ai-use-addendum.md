# Case: customer AI-use addendum

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (EU-headquartered freight forwarder; US entity signing). Annual fees $320,000. Smart Mapping suggests column mappings for shipment data; it does not make decisions about individuals.

**Playbook excerpt (AI terms):**
- Disclose AI features and AI model vendors (as subprocessors). 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a Model Vendor.
- No customer-run audits or testing of models or shared systems; offer documentation, evaluation summaries and the SOC 2 report under NDA.
- Comply with AI laws applicable to Provider. Do not accept contractual characterizations of Provider's regulatory classification or role; those are legal questions for counsel.

**Customer's AI Use Addendum:**
> 1. Provider will list its AI features and AI model vendors and give 30 days' notice before changing an AI model vendor.
> 2. Provider will not use AI in the Service to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.
> 3. Customer may annually conduct, or commission, bias and accuracy audits of Provider's AI models.
> 4. Provider acknowledges that Smart Mapping is a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act and will comply with all obligations of a provider of high-risk AI systems.
> 5. Customer may disable AI features for its account at any time.

**Ask:** Verdict on each item, what drives the decision, and the response.
