# Case: governing law moved to New York (enterprise prospect)

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise prospect, US-headquartered retailer, first-year contract value $180,000. This is the second turn of redlines on Provider's CSA. Provider's DPA for this deal (separately agreed) is governed by Irish law because the prospect's EU affiliates' personal data is in scope; that DPA is final.

**Playbook excerpt (governing law):** For enterprise customers, the CSA may be governed by the law of any US state with venue in US state or federal courts; the agreement owner may accept this without escalation. Non-US law or non-US venue for the CSA is outside the playbook. Standard customers keep §15 as published. The DPA may follow a different law, including non-US law, where data-protection law requires it.

**Incoming email (from the prospect's procurement lead):**
> I've attached our latest redlines, but the most substantive change is to the governing law, which has been changed to New York. If you reply to this email or send me back a note, I can send the final agreement over afterwards.

**Redline to §15 (supplied):** "This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of ~~Delaware~~ [New York], and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in ~~Delaware~~ [New York County, New York]."

The remaining changes in the returned draft are a compare-confirmed invoice-period change from quarterly to annual-in-advance and a customer logo-use restriction under the publicity clause; sales has already accepted both.

**Ask:** Is the governing-law change acceptable for Provider? Give the verdict, the reasoning, what would change the answer, and a short reply to procurement.
