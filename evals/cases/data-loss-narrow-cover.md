# Case: narrowed data-restoration carveout and long cover costs

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise customer (US staffing firm) on a three-year term with 30 months remaining. Annual fees $180,000.

**Playbook excerpt (damages exclusion):** acceptable carveouts from §11(c): reasonable costs of procuring replacement services for up to 12 months, and reasonable costs of restoring or reloading Customer Data from Provider's backups or Customer's source records, both subject to the applicable cap. Not acceptable: lost profits or revenue, or a general carveout for loss of data.

**Redline to §11(c):**
> ...except for indemnity obligations[, Customer's reasonable costs of restoring or reloading Customer Protected Data from Provider's backups or Customer's source records, and Customer's reasonable costs of procuring replacement services for the remainder of the Term, each subject to Section 11(a)].

**Ask:** Verdict on each element, the cap that applies, and a counter if needed.
