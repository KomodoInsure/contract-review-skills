# Case: AI off by default and per-user opt-in

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US regional bank's data-operations team). Annual fees $275,000. Product confirms Smart Mapping can be disabled per account today; there is no per-user setting, and adding one is on the roadmap with no committed date.

**Playbook excerpt (AI terms):** disclose AI features and AI model vendors; 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a Model Vendor; customers may disable AI features for their account. Do not commit to technical behavior the product does not support without product-owner confirmation.

**Customer redline (AI terms):**
> 1. [Smart Mapping and any future AI feature will be disabled for Customer's account by default and enabled only on Customer's written request.]
> 2. [Once enabled, each individual user must opt in before AI features process data that user submits.]
> 3. [Provider will give 30 days' notice before making any new AI feature available to Customer's account.]

**Ask:** Verdict on each item, what drives the decision, and the response.
