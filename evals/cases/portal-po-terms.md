# Case: procurement-portal terms on a purchase order

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Enterprise customer (US airline caterer) signed the CSA and an order form ($150,000 per year, net 30) last week. Its accounts-payable team now requires Provider to register in its procurement portal and accept a PO before any invoice is paid. The PO amount ($150,000) and term match the order form.

The portal's acceptance screen says: "By accepting this PO, Supplier agrees to Buyer's Standard Terms of Purchase, which govern and prevail over any other agreement between the parties." Those terms include unlimited supplier liability, net-90 payment and buyer ownership of all deliverables.

The account executive asks: "Can I click accept so we get paid, or do we need to redline the portal terms?"

**Ask:** Answer the account executive.
