# Case: data-loss and cover-cost carveouts from the damages exclusion

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US payroll bureau that imports client employee files). Annual fees $250,000.

**Playbook excerpt (damages exclusion):**
- Acceptable carveouts from §11(c): reasonable costs of procuring replacement services for up to 12 months, and reasonable costs of restoring or reloading Customer Data from Provider's backups or Customer's source records. Both remain subject to the applicable cap.
- Not acceptable: lost profits or revenue, or a general carveout for damages arising from loss of data.

**Redline to §11(c):**
> ...neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages or lost profits, except for indemnity obligations[, damages arising from loss or corruption of Customer Data, and Customer's costs of procuring replacement services].

No change to §11(a) or (b).

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, the cap that applies, and a counter.
