# Case: customer security schedule

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US insurance carrier). Annual fees $450,000. Provider's security team confirms Provider meets items (a) and (b) below today.

**Playbook excerpt (security commitments):**
- Agree to specific controls Provider meets today. Pen-test and SOC 2 reports are shared under NDA. No customer-run testing of shared systems.
- Provider must be able to update its Security Policy and controls if the update does not materially reduce the protection of Customer Protected Data.

**Customer's Information Security Schedule (proposed):**
> (a) AES-256 encryption at rest; TLS 1.2 or higher in transit.
> (b) MFA for all administrative access.
> (c) Provider will deliver its full annual penetration-test report within 10 days of completion, and Customer may conduct its own penetration test of the Service annually.
> (d) Provider shall not modify its security controls without Customer's prior written consent.
> (e) This Schedule forms part of Section 4 and prevails over the Security Policy.

**Ask:** Verdict on each item, what drives the decision, and the response.
