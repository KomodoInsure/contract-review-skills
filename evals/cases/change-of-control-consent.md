# Case: consent and termination on change of control

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New three-year enterprise agreement (US logistics marketplace). $200,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance. Provider is venture-backed.

**Playbook excerpt (assignment):**
- Keep §14.2's exception for assignment to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all assets. No consent requirement for a change of control.
- Fallback: Customer may terminate within 90 days after notice that Provider has been acquired by a direct competitor of Customer, with a refund of prepaid fees for the unused part of the term.

**Redline to §14.2:**
> ...~~except either party may assign without consent to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all its assets~~. [Any change of control of Provider is an assignment requiring Customer's prior written consent. Customer may terminate this Agreement within 90 days after any change of control of Provider and receive a refund of all prepaid fees.]

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, and a counter. Illustrate the refund difference if a change of control happens six months into a contract year.
