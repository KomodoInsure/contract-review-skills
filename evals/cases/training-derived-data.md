# Case: no-training commitment that also reaches derived data

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US health-benefits administrator; no PHI is in scope). Annual fees $180,000. The customer's security team asked whether Provider trains AI on customer data.

**Playbook excerpt (data use and AI):**
- Provider may commit in writing not to train or fine-tune any AI model on Protected Data, and may limit its processing of Protected Data to providing the Service to Customer. The agreement owner may accept these edits to §4.
- Provider's rights in Metadata under §4 must be preserved; Metadata is how Smart Mapping is improved.

**Redline to §4:**
> Provider processes Protected Data only to provide ~~and improve~~ the Service [to Customer]. [Provider shall not use Customer Protected Data, or any data derived from it, to train, fine-tune or improve any artificial intelligence model or any other product or service.]
>
> "Customer Protected Data" means data Customer submits to the Service[, including all data derived from such data].

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Can Provider accept this? Give the verdict on each change, identify what drives the decision, and propose language.
