# Case: customer-run audit clause in the DPA (new customer)

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer, US-only healthcare-adjacent analytics company (not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate for this use; no PHI in scope), contract value $150,000. Data in scope: US business contact records. No EU/UK personal data and no regulated financial-services activity. The customer is not subject to any regulator audit-access requirement for this service.

**Playbook excerpt (audits and security assurance):**
- Provider meets audit and compliance-demonstration obligations through its annual independent SOC 2 Type II audit, by an auditor Provider selects, and its annual third-party penetration test. Reports are shared under NDA. Provider will complete one reasonable security questionnaire per year.
- Provider does not grant individual customers rights to conduct their own audits, inspections or technical testing, or to choose the auditor, because every customer shares the same infrastructure and an independent audit already serves all of them.
- Alternatives the agreement owner may offer: the latest SOC 2 report and pen-test summary under NDA; prioritizing a specific certification the customer needs on Provider's compliance roadmap; dedicated (single-tenant) hosting at additional cost.
- Where law applicable to the customer requires direct audit or regulator access, escalate to outside counsel.

**Incoming email (from the customer's CEO):**
> Looking forward to working with y'all! Our team is loving the product, but an additional ask on the process. See the attached updated DPA.

**Provider's standard DPA §6.5:** "Provider shall make available to Customer, on request and under confidentiality, its most recent independent SOC 2 Type II report and penetration test summary, and shall respond to one reasonable security questionnaire per year."

**Customer's replacement §6.5:**
> 6.5. Vendor shall provide Customer with all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, or other technical and operational testing conducted by Customer or an auditor mandated by Customer at least once every twelve (12) months.

No other DPA change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Should Provider accept the new §6.5? Give the verdict, the reasoning, what would change it, and how to respond to the customer.
