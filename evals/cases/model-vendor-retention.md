# Case: DPA subprocessor terms that reach the model vendor

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US consumer-lending platform). Annual fees $250,000. Smart Mapping is part of what the customer bought.

**Playbook excerpt (subprocessors):**
- 30 days' notice of new subprocessors. If the customer objects on reasonable data-protection grounds, the parties discuss in good faith; if unresolved, the customer may terminate the affected Service and receive a refund of prepaid, unused fees as its sole remedy. No customer veto over a subprocessor of the shared service.
- Subprocessor commitments must match what subprocessors actually do; confirm technical facts with the product owner.

**DPA redline (subprocessors section):**
> (a) Provider will give [90] ~~30~~ days' notice of any new subprocessor. [If Customer objects, Provider shall not use that subprocessor to process Customer Personal Data.]
> (b) [No subprocessor will store or retain Customer Personal Data for any period after processing of the relevant request is complete.]
> (c) [No subprocessor will use Customer Personal Data to train artificial intelligence models.]

**Ask:** Verdict on each change, what drives the decision, and what Provider can offer.
