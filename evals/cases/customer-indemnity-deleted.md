# Case: customer deletes its indemnity

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US marketing-analytics firm that imports purchased consumer contact lists). Annual fees $210,000.

**Playbook excerpt (customer indemnity):**
- §12.2 may be narrowed to third-party claims that Customer Protected Data, or Provider's processing of it in accordance with the Agreement, infringes a third party's rights or violates law (including collection without required notice or consent), and claims arising from Customer's breach of the Acceptable Use Policy.
- It may be made subject to the same cap treatment as §12.1. It may not be deleted.

**Redline:**
> ~~§12.2 Customer defends Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer Protected Data or Customer's breach of the Acceptable Use Policy.~~ [Each party is responsible for its own acts and omissions.]

Customer comment: "We don't give indemnities to vendors. Your indemnity covers your product; ours shouldn't exist."

**Ask:** Verdict, driver and response.
