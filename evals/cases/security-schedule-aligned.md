# Case: a security schedule aligned with policy

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US health-tech company; no PHI in scope). Annual fees $380,000. Provider's security team confirms it meets items (a)–(c) today.

**Playbook excerpt (security commitments):** agree to specific controls Provider meets today; pen-test and SOC 2 reports shared under NDA; no customer-run testing of shared systems; Provider may update controls if the update does not materially reduce protection of Customer Protected Data.

**Customer's Information Security Schedule (proposed):**
> (a) AES-256 encryption at rest; TLS 1.2 or higher in transit.
> (b) MFA for all administrative access; access reviews at least quarterly.
> (c) Annual third-party penetration test; Provider will share the executive summary under NDA on request.
> (d) Provider may update its controls if the update does not materially reduce the protection of Customer Protected Data, and will notify Customer of any material change.
> (e) This Schedule forms part of Section 4. If it conflicts with the Security Policy, the more protective provision applies.

**Ask:** Verdict on each item, anything to watch, and the reply.
