# Case: SLA uplift and a termination trigger

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US travel booking platform). Annual fees $280,000. SLA terms are commercial; sales owns them, with the guidance below.

**Sales guidance (SLA):**
- Availability target stays 99.9% monthly. Service credits up to 25% of monthly fees remain the sole remedy for unavailability.
- Fallback: Customer may terminate for chronic failure (below 99.9% in three consecutive months, or in any four months in twelve) with a refund of prepaid, unused fees. Other changes need business-owner approval.

**Redline to §13:**
> Availability: ~~99.9%~~ [99.99%] monthly. Service credits up to 25% of the monthly fee ~~are Customer's sole remedy for unavailability~~ [are in addition to any other remedies]. [Customer may terminate this Agreement if availability falls below 99.5% in any month.]

**Ask:** The account executive asks which of these can be accepted. Give verdicts, what drives the decision, the downtime each target allows, and a counter.
