# Case: uncapped indemnities including a new breach-of-agreement indemnity

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US insurance brokerage). Annual fees $300,000, paid annually in advance.

**Playbook excerpt (indemnity caps):**
- For enterprise customers, Provider's §12.1 IP infringement indemnity may be excluded from the §11(a) cap (uncapped). The agreement owner may accept this.
- Any other Provider indemnity is subject to a separate cap of up to 3.0x trailing-12-month fees, with or without a floor up to $1,000,000, within Provider's insurance limit. Each indemnity must cover third-party claims arising from a defined event.
- Provider does not indemnify for its own breach of the Agreement generally. Anything outside these positions escalates to the business owner.

**Redline:**
> §11(a): ...in the 12 months before the claim[, except Provider's obligations under Section 12, which are not subject to any limitation].
>
> [§12.3 Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer and its affiliates from and against all losses, damages, costs and expenses arising out of or relating to Provider's breach of this Agreement.]

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Should Provider accept? Give the verdict on each change, identify what drives the decision, and draft the counter and a short note to the customer.
