# Case: an insurance clause that matches coverage

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US logistics broker). Annual fees $350,000. §11 unchanged (Increased Claims at 5.0x).

**Playbook excerpt (insurance):** describe coverage Provider actually carries by type and limit; certificates on request; additional insured on CGL only; do not commit to limits Provider does not carry; do not represent that insurance covers any particular liability.

**Redline (new §16):**
> [§16 Insurance. During the Term Provider shall maintain: (a) combined cyber liability and technology errors and omissions insurance with a limit of at least $5,000,000 in the annual aggregate; (b) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, naming Customer as an additional insured. Provider shall provide certificates of insurance on request. Provider's insurance does not limit its liability under this Agreement.]

**Ask:** Can Provider accept §16? Verdict on each element, and anything to watch.
