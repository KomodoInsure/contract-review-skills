# Case: customer insurance requirements and a coverage question

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US fintech lender). Annual fees $600,000. §11 is unchanged: the Increased Claims cap stays at 5.0x trailing fees.

**Playbook excerpt (insurance):**
- An insurance clause may describe the coverage Provider actually carries, by type and limit. Provide certificates on request.
- Customers may be named as additional insureds on CGL only.
- Do not commit to limits or policy terms Provider does not carry. Buying additional coverage is a business-owner decision, priced with the broker.
- Do not represent that insurance covers any particular liability.

**Redline (new §16):**
> [§16 Insurance. Provider shall maintain throughout the Term: (a) cyber liability insurance of at least $10,000,000 per claim; (b) technology errors and omissions insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; (c) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate. Customer shall be named as an additional insured on all policies. All policies shall be primary and non-contributory and include a waiver of subrogation in favour of Customer. Provider shall give Customer 30 days' notice of cancellation.]

The customer's counsel also writes: "Please confirm Provider's insurance covers its liability under this Agreement."

**Ask:** What should Provider do with §16 and the question? Give verdicts, identify the driver, and draft the reply.
