# Case: a standard-tier customer asks for a no-training clause

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** Standard-tier customer (US nonprofit, self-serve plan, $24,000 a year). Its operations lead sends the same redline other enterprise customers have proposed, adding to §4: "Provider shall not use Customer Protected Data to train or fine-tune any artificial intelligence model."

**Playbook excerpt (data use and AI):**
- Provider may commit in writing not to train or fine-tune AI models on Protected Data (enterprise contracts).
- Standard customers sign the CSA as published; no legal-term redlines. Provider's public Trust Center states that Provider does not train AI models on customer data and lists its subprocessors.
