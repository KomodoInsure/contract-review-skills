# Case: audit and regulator access for an EU-regulated bank

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer, a German-licensed bank, contract value $320,000. The bank will use the Service to onboard corporate-client records containing EU personal data as part of a process its compliance team classifies as supporting a critical or important function. Provider is the bank's processor for that personal data and an ICT third-party service provider to it.

**Playbook excerpt (audits and security assurance):**
- Provider meets audit and compliance-demonstration obligations through its annual independent SOC 2 Type II audit, by an auditor Provider selects, and its annual third-party penetration test. Reports are shared under NDA. Provider will complete one reasonable security questionnaire per year.
- Provider does not grant individual customers rights to conduct their own audits, inspections or technical testing, or to choose the auditor, because every customer shares the same infrastructure and an independent audit already serves all of them.
- Alternatives the agreement owner may offer: the latest SOC 2 report and pen-test summary under NDA; prioritizing a specific certification; dedicated (single-tenant) hosting at additional cost.
- Where law applicable to the customer requires direct audit or regulator access, escalate to outside counsel.

**Bank's proposed new DPA §6.6 (in addition to Provider's standard §6.5 reports clause):**
> 6.6. Vendor shall grant Customer, its internal and external auditors, and its competent supervisory authorities full access to and audit and inspection rights over Vendor's relevant premises, systems, data and documentation, including the right to take copies, as required by applicable financial-services regulation. Customer shall give reasonable notice of audits other than those required by an authority or following a Security Incident.

No other change (compare-confirmed).

**Ask:** Should Provider accept §6.6? Give the verdict, the reasoning, what would change it, and next steps.
