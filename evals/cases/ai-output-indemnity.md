# Case: IP indemnity for AI outputs

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US software company) that plans to use Smart Mapping heavily. Annual fees $350,000. The customer's counsel: "Every AI vendor we sign now covers outputs."

**Playbook excerpt (AI outputs):**
- Provider does not offer an output indemnity as standard.
- The business owner may approve a conditional output indemnity that does not go beyond Model Vendor's protection to Provider: copyright claims only, unmodified outputs, excluding claims arising from Customer inputs or Protected Data and from combinations, subject to §11(a). The agreement owner may propose it subject to that approval.

**Redline (new §12.1A):**
> [Provider will defend and indemnify Customer against any third-party claim alleging that any output of Smart Mapping, or Customer's use of such output, infringes or misappropriates any intellectual property right.]

**Ask:** Verdict, driver, who must approve, and counter language.
