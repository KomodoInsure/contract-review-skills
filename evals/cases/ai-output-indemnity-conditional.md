# Case: a customer proposes a conditional AI output indemnity

Read `_provider-context.md` first; it is part of this packet.

**Deal:** New enterprise customer (US marketing agency). Annual fees $260,000.

**Playbook excerpt (AI outputs):** Provider does not offer an output indemnity as standard. The business owner may approve a conditional output indemnity that does not go beyond Model Vendor's protection to Provider: copyright claims only, unmodified outputs, excluding claims arising from Customer inputs or Protected Data and from combinations, subject to §11(a). The agreement owner may propose it subject to that approval.

**Redline (new §12.1A):**
> [Provider will defend Customer against third-party claims that an unmodified output of Smart Mapping infringes a copyright, and pay resulting damages and approved settlements, subject to Section 11(a). This Section does not apply to claims arising from Customer Protected Data or other inputs Customer provides, or from combination of the output with materials not supplied by Provider.]

**Ask:** Should Provider accept? Verdict, what (if anything) is still needed, and the reply.
