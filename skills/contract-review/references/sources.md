# Authority and limits

This skill is original synthesis, not a company playbook or a substitute for current jurisdiction-specific authority. Public forms and drafting commentary are reference material, not binding law or approved negotiating positions. Verify the operative version and redistribution rights before reusing source text. For a legal conclusion, identify the jurisdiction, relevant facts, exact authority and date, currentness check, and what the authority supports. Label secondary commentary and unresolved questions; do not cite a research register as proof of a legal requirement.
