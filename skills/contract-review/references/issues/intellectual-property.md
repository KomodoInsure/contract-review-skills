# Intellectual property and usable rights

Inventory background technology, custom deliverables, third-party components, customer materials, feedback, usage data and outputs. Identify ownership, license scope, timing, payment conditions and retained rights separately.

“Customer owns deliverables” does not answer whether embedded tools can be used, modified, maintained or transferred. Map the rights required for the intended use and compare license duration, territory, users, sublicensing, derivatives and post-termination survival. A license can solve usability without assigning the supplier's pre-existing platform.

For provider review, prevent an overbroad definition of work product from inadvertently transferring reusable tools or general improvements when the approved objective is to reserve them. For customer review, avoid a reservation that makes paid-for deliverables unusable. The correct allocation depends on the mandate, not a universal preference for either ownership or licensing.

Resolve feedback and usage-data permissions against confidentiality and customer-content definitions. A broad feedback license may matter if confidential designs are treated as feedback. Do not assume an ownership clause creates a right to process personal data or overrides confidentiality.

For third-party and open-source components, request the relevant inventory and license information if the requested conclusion depends on it. Do not infer clean title, sublicensing rights or absence of restrictions from the supplier's warranty alone.

Check infringement indemnity, defense, modification/replacement remedies and termination/refund together with the license. Product replacement that materially impairs contracted functionality can fail the business objective even if it avoids infringement.

Use source-specific legal research for assignment formalities, moral rights, employee/contractor ownership, database rights and AI output protection when material. Do not promise that contractual ownership language creates statutory IP rights where none exist.
