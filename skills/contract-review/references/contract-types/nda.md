# Confidentiality agreements

Establish the purpose, discloser/recipient roles, whether disclosure is mutual in substance, term, covered information and representatives. A mutual document can still create asymmetric exposure when only one party discloses valuable material.

Check oral/visual information, marking requirements, information disclosed before signing, independent development, prior knowledge, public information and lawful third-party sources. Distinguish an exception from a right to use protected information. Do not delete an independent-development exception solely because it resembles a competitive-use right.

Use restrictions, permitted disclosure, security and responsibility for representatives are distinct. An affiliate allowed to receive information is not automatically a party entitled to enforce every provision. Check whether disclosures to AI providers fit permitted recipients and purpose; no-training language alone does not settle this.

Separate disclosure period, agreement duration and survival of protection. Trade-secret treatment may need jurisdiction-specific analysis; do not state a universal mandatory confidentiality duration.

Evaluate residuals, feedback and similar-development provisions against the intended information exchange. A residuals clause can materially change protection even if the confidentiality promise remains. Prefer a targeted resolution over automatically banning all use of general skills.

Return/deletion duties must address routine backups, legal retention, permitted access and continued protection. If feasible retention is unknown, request operational evidence before proposing a precise deadline.

Examine non-solicitation, exclusivity, noncompete, IP transfer and publicity provisions on their substance even if hidden in an NDA. They may exceed the mandate. An injunctive-relief clause does not establish that a court will automatically grant relief.
